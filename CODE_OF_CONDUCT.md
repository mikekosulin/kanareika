# Code of conduct

Kanareika is a small project, and we want it to stay a place where anyone can report a bug, ask a question or send a fix without being put down for it. This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (CC BY 4.0).

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, including with people new to security, Go or open source.
- Disagree about the code, not about the person. Say what you would do instead.
- Accept feedback, give credit, and own your mistakes.
- Keep other people's infrastructure private: no real hosts, addresses or findings in issues, as [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) says.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or sexualized language and imagery.
- Attacks on someone's identity: age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation.
- Publishing someone's private information, or a vulnerability in someone's systems, without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, and anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commit messages) and wherever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainer privately through the contact on [github.com/mikekosulin](https://github.com/mikekosulin). Reports are read promptly and kept confidential; the reporter is never penalized for raising a concern in good faith. Security vulnerabilities in Kanareika go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens

Depending on what happened: a private note explaining what was wrong; a warning with a time-limited restriction on interacting with the project; a temporary ban; or, for repeated or serious violations, a permanent ban. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this code.
